中文版

一、我们收集什么

我们不收集、不上传、不存储任何个人身份信息。
  1. 字幕文本：沉浸式翻译和页面可读取的标准英文字幕在浏览器本地处理。YouTube 原生字幕兜底会向 YouTube 请求所选英文字幕轨，可能下载整条字幕并包含尚未播放的部分。我们没有服务器，字幕不会上传给本扩展运营方；YouTube 请求仍受其自身服务条款和隐私政策约束。
  2. 单词查询：当你主动点击某个单词查看释义时，该单词会被发送至以下公开词典接口：
    • api.dictionaryapi.dev（Free Dictionary API）
    • en.wiktionary.org（维基词典）
    查询词会出现在请求地址中；扩展不会附带当前视频字幕或视频页面地址。第三方服务可能按其政策处理请求和常规网络元数据。
  3. 测试题与朗读：测试题数据随扩展本地打包，选择和判分在本地完成。单词朗读只使用浏览器标记为本地服务的英语音色；没有可用音色时扩展会提示，不会选择远程音色。
  4. 本地设置和记录：所选词库、学习模式、熟悉词、匹配词卡、时间点和历史记录保存在浏览器本地存储（chrome.storage.local）。原始字幕不会作为长期记录保存。页面仍打开时，尚未获得本地保存确认的短期输入缓存保留在页面内存中；页面与浏览器同时突然关闭前的最后一小段未确认输入可能丢失。
  5. 本地计时：字幕回调、匹配、保存和弹窗更新耗时可保存在本地内存中用于调试，不上传遥测或分析。

二、我们不做什么

  • ❌ 不读取或上传浏览器浏览记录；扩展只在本地保存自己采集过的视频记录
  • ❌ 不收集个人身份信息（姓名、邮箱、电话等）
  • ❌ 不使用 Cookie 追踪
  • ❌ 不植入广告
  • ❌ 不进行数据统计分析
  • ❌ 不将任何数据出售或分享给第三方（除上文第 2 条所述的必要单词查询外）

三、权限用途说明

权限用途
storage在本地保存你标记的「已斩词」和词汇记录
tabs与当前视频页面通信，获取已收集的字幕词汇
主机权限在匹配的视频页面读取可见字幕和可访问的标准 TextTrack，并向 YouTube 请求当前视频的字幕轨

四、数据安全

字幕匹配和历史记录合并在本地完成。对外网络请求包括 YouTube 原生字幕兜底所需的字幕轨请求，以及你主动点击单词时发起的词典查询。标准字幕轨与沉浸式翻译文本不上传。

五、第三方服务

本扩展使用以下公开服务，其数据处理受各自隐私政策约束：

  • YouTube — 原生英文字幕兜底按当前播放器提供的字幕轨地址请求内容，可能请求整条字幕轨。YouTube 隐私政策
  • Free Dictionary API — dictionaryapi.dev
  • Wiktionary — Wikimedia Privacy Policy

六、儿童隐私

本扩展不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不收集任何年龄相关信息。

七、政策变更

如本政策有更新，我们会在本页面更新「最后更新」日期。

八、联系方式

如有疑问或想反馈问题，欢迎到 GitHub Issues 提交，我们会尽快回复。

English

1. What We Collect

We do not collect, upload, or store any personally identifiable information.
  1. Subtitle text: Immersive Translate and readable standard English text tracks are processed locally. The YouTube native-caption fallback requests the selected English caption track from YouTube and may download the entire track, including unplayed sections. We have no server; subtitles are not uploaded to the extension operator. YouTube requests remain subject to YouTube's own terms and privacy policy.
  2. Word lookups: When you manually click a word, the query appears in requests to api.dictionaryapi.dev and, if needed, en.wiktionary.org. The extension does not include the current video's subtitles or page URL. Those services may process the request and ordinary network metadata under their policies.
  3. Quiz and speech: Quiz data is bundled with the extension and answers are checked locally. Speech uses only English voices that the browser identifies as local services; if none are available, the extension displays a notice and does not select a remote voice.
  4. Local settings and records: Your selected vocabulary, learning mode, familiar-word list, matched word cards, timestamps, and history are stored in chrome.storage.local. Raw subtitles are not kept as long-term records. While the page remains open, unconfirmed input is held temporarily in page memory; the last unconfirmed portion may be lost if the page and browser close unexpectedly at the same time.
  5. Local timing: Capture, matching, saving, and popup-update timings may be kept in local memory for debugging. They are not uploaded as telemetry or analytics.

2. What We Do Not Do

  • ❌ Does not read or upload browser browsing history; it keeps only its own captured-video records locally
  • ❌ No personal information collection
  • ❌ No tracking cookies
  • ❌ No advertising
  • ❌ No analytics
  • ❌ No selling or sharing of data with third parties (except the necessary word lookup described above)

3. Permissions

4. Data Security

Subtitle matching and history merging are done locally. Outbound requests include YouTube caption-track retrieval for the native fallback and dictionary lookups after a manual word click. Immersive Translate text and standard text tracks are not uploaded.

5. Third-Party Services

  • YouTube — the native English-caption fallback requests the current player's caption track and may retrieve the full track. YouTube Privacy Policy
  • Free Dictionary API — dictionaryapi.dev
  • Wiktionary — Wikimedia Privacy Policy

6. Children's Privacy

This extension is not directed at children under 13 and collects no age-related information.

7. Changes

Any updates to this policy will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above.

8. Contact

For questions or bug reports, please open an issue at GitHub Issues. We will get back to you as soon as possible.
