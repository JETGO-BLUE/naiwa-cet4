# 隐私政策（Privacy Policy）

**扩展名称：奶娃过四级（Naiwa CET-4 Collector）**
**最后更新：2026 年 10 月 12 日**

---

## 中文版

### 一、我们收集什么

**我们不收集、不上传、不存储任何个人身份信息。**

具体说明：

1. **字幕文本**：沉浸式翻译和页面可读取的标准英文字幕在浏览器本地处理。YouTube 原生字幕兜底会向 YouTube 请求所选英文字幕轨，可能下载整条字幕并包含尚未播放的部分。我们没有服务器，字幕不会上传给本扩展运营方；YouTube 请求仍受 YouTube 自身的服务条款和隐私政策约束。
2. **单词查询**：当你**主动点击**某个单词查看释义时，该单词会被发送至以下公开词典接口以获取释义：
   - `api.dictionaryapi.dev`（Free Dictionary API）
   - `en.wiktionary.org`（维基词典）
   查询词会出现在请求地址中；扩展不会附带当前视频字幕或视频页面地址。第三方服务可能按其政策处理请求和常规网络元数据。
3. **测试题与朗读**：测试题数据随扩展本地打包，选项和判定在本地完成。单词朗读只使用浏览器标记为本地服务的英语音色；没有可用音色时提示，不会选择远程音色。
4. **本地设置和记录**：你标记为"已斩"（熟悉词）的单词列表、匹配词卡、时间点及历史记录保存在浏览器本地存储（`chrome.storage.local`）中。原始字幕不会作为长期记录保存。页面仍打开时，尚未获得本地保存确认的短期输入缓存保留在页面内存中；页面与浏览器同时突然关闭前的最后一小段未确认输入可能丢失。
5. **本地计时**：扩展可在内存中记录字幕回调、匹配、保存和弹窗更新耗时，用于本地调试；不上传遥测或分析。

### 二、我们不做什么

- ❌ 不读取或上传浏览器浏览记录；扩展只在本地保存自己采集过的视频记录
- ❌ 不收集个人身份信息（姓名、邮箱、电话等）
- ❌ 不使用 Cookie 追踪
- ❌ 不植入广告
- ❌ 不进行数据统计分析
- ❌ 不将任何数据出售或分享给第三方（除上文第 2 条所述的必要单词查询外）

### 三、权限用途说明

| 权限 | 用途 |
|------|------|
| `storage` | 在本地保存你标记的"已斩词"和词汇记录 |
| `tabs` | 与当前视频页面通信，获取已收集的字幕词汇 |
| `主机权限` | 在匹配的视频页面读取可见字幕和可访问的标准 TextTrack，并向 YouTube 请求当前视频的字幕轨 |

### 四、数据安全

字幕文字匹配和历史记录合并在本地完成。对外网络请求包括 YouTube 原生字幕兜底所需的字幕轨请求，以及你主动点击单词时发起的词典查询。标准字幕轨与沉浸式翻译文本不上传。

### 五、第三方服务

本扩展使用以下公开服务，其数据处理受各自隐私政策约束：
- YouTube — 原生英文字幕兜底按页面提供的字幕轨地址请求内容，可能请求整条字幕轨。
- Free Dictionary API — https://dictionaryapi.dev/
- Wiktionary — https://foundation.wikimedia.org/wiki/Policy:Privacy_policy

### 六、儿童隐私

本扩展不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不收集任何年龄相关信息。

### 七、政策变更

如本政策有更新，我们会在本页面更新"最后更新"日期。

### 八、联系方式

如有疑问或想反馈问题，欢迎到 GitHub Issues 提交：

## English Version

### 1. What We Collect

**We do not collect, upload, or store personally identifiable information.**

1. **Subtitle text**: Immersive Translate and readable standard English text tracks are processed locally. The YouTube native-caption fallback requests the selected English caption track from YouTube and may download the entire track, including unplayed sections. We have no server; subtitles are not uploaded to the extension operator. YouTube requests remain subject to YouTube's own terms and privacy policy.
2. **Word lookups**: When you manually click a word, the query word appears in requests to `api.dictionaryapi.dev` and, if needed, `en.wiktionary.org`. The extension does not include the current video's subtitles or page URL. Those services may process the request and ordinary network metadata under their policies.
3. **Quiz and speech**: Quiz data is bundled with the extension and answers are checked locally. Speech uses only English voices that the browser identifies as local services; if none are available, the extension displays a notice and does not select a remote voice.
4. **Local settings and records**: Your familiar-word list, matched word cards, timestamps, and history are stored in `chrome.storage.local`. Raw subtitles are not kept as long-term records. While the page remains open, unconfirmed input is held temporarily in page memory; the last unconfirmed portion may be lost if the page and browser close unexpectedly at the same time.
5. **Local timing**: Capture, matching, saving, and popup-update timings may be kept in local memory for debugging. They are not uploaded as telemetry or analytics.

### 2. What We Do Not Do

- ❌ Does not read or upload browser browsing history; it keeps only its own captured-video records locally
- ❌ No personal information collection
- ❌ No tracking cookies
- ❌ No advertising
- ❌ No analytics
- ❌ No selling or sharing of data with third parties (except the necessary word lookup described above)

### 3. Permissions

| Permission | Purpose |
|------------|---------|
| `storage` | Save your slashed words and vocabulary records locally |
| `tabs` | Communicate with the current video page to retrieve collected words |
| `Host permissions` | Read visible subtitles and accessible standard text tracks on matching video pages, and request the current video's subtitle track from YouTube |

### 4. Data Security

Subtitle matching and history merging are done locally. Outbound requests include YouTube caption-track retrieval for the native fallback and dictionary lookups after a manual word click. Immersive Translate text and standard text tracks are not uploaded.

### 5. Third-Party Services

- YouTube — the native English-caption fallback requests the current player's caption track and may retrieve the full track.
- Free Dictionary API — https://dictionaryapi.dev/
- Wiktionary — https://foundation.wikimedia.org/wiki/Policy:Privacy_policy

### 6. Children's Privacy

This extension is not directed at children under 13 and collects no age-related information.

### 7. Changes

Any updates to this policy will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above.
