### 详细描述（Description）

```
【奶娃过四级】——看视频的时候，把四级词汇悄悄攒起来。

你每天刷的视频，其实藏着大量四级词汇。可惜看完就忘，一个都没留下。

这个扩展帮你把它们捞出来：

【使用前提】请务必打开「沉浸式翻译」的双语字幕（英文一行 + 中文一行）。
本扩展读取的就是这套双语字幕里的英文原文 —— 不开的话，除 YouTube 自带字幕外，
Netflix / B站 这类平台基本抓不到词。「沉浸式翻译」是另一个免费扩展，商店可搜到。

▎自动抓词
播放视频时，自动从字幕里提取属于四级范围的单词（默认读取「沉浸式翻译」的双语字幕，未开启时回退到 YouTube 原生字幕）。

▎标注时间点
每个词都记录它在视频里出现的位置（mm:ss），最多显示前 10 个，方便你回看原句。

▎一键「斩」
认识？点一下「斩」，这个词以后就不再出现。越用越清爽，专注那些你还不熟的词。

▎点词看详情
点击任意单词，立刻看到：
· 单词变形——复数、过去式、过去分词、现在分词、比较级、最高级，本地计算，秒出
· 各词性释义——只保留常用释义，偏门解释自动过滤
· 原型/词根与同根词

▎完全不打扰
纯只读监听页面字幕，不修改任何页面内容，不影响翻译扩展，不影响你看视频。

▎隐私优先
字幕在你电脑本地处理，不上传；只有你主动点词查释义时，才把那个单词发给公开词典接口。无需登录，无广告，无追踪。

——
适合：备考四六级的同学、想在看视频时顺带积累词汇的人。
```

## 三、English Listing（两个商店都建议补一份）

### Name
```
Naiwa CET-4 Collector
```

### Summary
```
Collect CET-4 vocabulary while watching videos: auto-extract CET-4 words from subtitles, mark timestamps, view word forms, and slash words you already know.
```

### Description
```
Naiwa CET-4 Collector — quietly build your CET-4 vocabulary while watching videos.

Every video you watch is full of CET-4 vocabulary. This extension picks those words out and keeps them for you.

REQUIREMENT
Please turn on Immersive Translate's bilingual subtitles (English + Chinese). The extension reads the
English lines from those subtitles — without them it can only fall back to YouTube's native captions,
and will find nothing on Netflix or Bilibili. Immersive Translate is a separate free extension.

FEATURES
▎Auto word extraction
Extracts CET-4 words from the subtitles as you watch. It reads Immersive Translate's bilingual subtitles by default, and falls back to YouTube's native captions when they are not enabled.

▎Timestamps
Each word is recorded with its positions in the video (mm:ss), up to the first 10, so you can jump back to the original sentence.

▎One-click "Slash"
Already know a word? Slash it, and it stops appearing — just like the familiar-word logic in Chinese vocabulary apps.

▎Click any word for details
· Word forms — plural, past tense, past participle, present participle, comparative and superlative, computed locally and instantly
· Part-of-speech meanings — common meanings only, obscure senses filtered out
· Base form / root and related words

▎Non-intrusive
Read-only observation of subtitle elements. It never modifies the page, never interferes with translation extensions, and never affects playback.

▎Privacy first
Subtitles are processed locally on your device and never uploaded. Only the single word you click is sent to public dictionary APIs. No sign-in, no ads, no tracking.

PERMISSIONS EXPLAINED
· storage — saves your slashed words and vocabulary records locally
· tabs — communicates with the current video page to collect words
· host permissions — reads subtitle text displayed on video pages

WHO IT'S FOR
Students preparing for CET-4/CET-6, and anyone who wants to pick up vocabulary passively while watching videos.
```
